Partnerships are typically structured as annual programs — 12 months from your start date, in any season. There’s no enrollment window to wait for: pick your start date and the curriculum, newsletter, and workshops roll with your calendar.

The building blocks of a partnership

Coaches’ Education Curriculum

Full access for your entire staff to the 11-module coaches’ education course — how to build mental skills into practice, spot warning signs early, and have the hard conversations with athletes.

Weekly Coaches’ Newsletter

A short, practical weekly brief for your coaches — one mental skill, one thing to watch for, one thing to try at practice this week.

Athlete & Team Workshops

Interactive workshops with your teams on preparation, focus under pressure, and honest review — delivered in person or over Zoom, 45–90 minutes.

Consulting Availability

Direct access to a licensed therapist & mental performance consultant when situations come up with individual athletes, staff, or teams — so nobody is improvising in a hard moment.

How these combine depends on your tier below — every tier is department-wide, whether you have 10 coaches or 100. Virtual partnerships are available nationwide; in-person partnerships start at the $20,000 level.
